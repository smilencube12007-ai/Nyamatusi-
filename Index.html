Our story

Nyamatusi Camp is our flagship in terms of its Eco-conscious style. Our Mana pools safari lodge is set in one of the wildest and most remote parts of Africa, on the banks of the Zambezi, one of the world's oldest rivers. Embracing comfort, this property embodies sustainable design. Fully solar-powered and built from all-natural materials, it encompasses its wilderness location in the most elegant way. Here, your Mana pools safari plays out against a sweeping riverine landscape, wjhere epic encounters of the giants that roam this land await.

Our activities

Game drives

Game drives are what gets us up in the morning in the African bush. They are a great avenue for exploring the land in a safe and exciting way.

Walking safari

Explore the bush on foot with our expert guides. Get up close to nature and learn to track wildlife.

Bird watching

Mana Pools is home to over 400 bird species. Perfect for bird lovers with binoculars and guide books.

Canoeing

Paddle down the Zambezi river and see elephants, hippos and crocodiles from the water.

Fishing

Try tiger fishing on the Zambezi - one of the most exciting freshwater fishing experiences.

Recent wildlife sightings

Wildlife sightings include elephant, kudu, sable antelope, zebra, wildebeest, the rare and endangered roan antelope, buffalo, lion, leopard, hyena, and many other wildlife species.
